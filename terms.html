Terms & Conditions

Darzi Khata istemaal karne se pehle in shuraait ko dhyan se parhein.

1. Platform ka Kirdaar

Darzi Khata ek connecting platform hai jo tailoring shops, customers, aur workers (karigars) ko aapas mein rabta karne ki sahulat deta hai. Hum khud koi tailoring service, mulazmat (job), ya mal (goods) faraham nahi karte.

2. Zimmedari (Liability)

Kisi bhi mulazmat, kaam, khareed-o-farokht, ya mulaqaat ka faisla aur uski zimmedari mukammal taur par dono fareeqain (users) ki apni hoti hai. Darzi Khata kisi bhi maali nuksan, fraud, jismani nuksan, ya kisi bhi qisam ke dispute ka zimmedar nahi hoga jo platform ke zariye hue rabte se paida ho.

3. Safety Advisory

Kisi naye rabte se milne se pehle, khaas taur par mahfooz na hone wale ilaqon mein, poori tasdeeq kar lein aur apne kisi qareebi ya ghar wale ko apni location aur mulaqaat ki tafseel zaroor batayein. Platform kisi mulaqaat ki tajweez, arrange, ya guarantee nahi karta.

4. Data aur Tasdeeq

Signup ke waqt diya gaya CNIC number sirf record ke maqasid ke liye rakha jata hai aur kisi doosre user ko nahi dikhaya jata. Ye number is waqt independently verify nahi kiya jata (jab tak ke aap ko alag se bataya na jaye), is liye har user ko khud apni tasdeeq/tahqeeq karni chahiye.

5. Account Suspension

Agar koi user fraud, jhoothi maloomat, ya kisi doosre user ko nuksan pohanchane ki koshish kare, to Darzi Khata us account ko bina pehle se ittila diye block karne ka haq mehfooz rakhta hai. Block hone ke baad CNIC bhi platform se dobara account banane se rok diya jata hai.

6. Payments

Subscription aur placement fees manual tareeqe se (JazzCash/EasyPaisa/Bank) jama ki jati hain aur admin dwara verify hone ke baad account active hota hai. Payment na hone ki soorat mein access mehdood ho sakti hai.

7. Reporting

Agar koi user ko kisi bhi qisam ka fraud, dhoka, ya ghalat sulook mehsoos ho, to wo turant "Report" button ke zariye ittila de sakta hai. Har report ki jaanch ki jayegi.

Ye shuraait waqtan faoqtan tabdeel ho sakti hain. Platform istemaal karke aap in shuraait se ittefaq karte hain.
